Delete your Talaiot account

You can delete your Talaiot account and all associated data at any time, directly from the app.

From the app (immediate)

  • Open Talaiot and go to Settings (the gear at the top right).
  • In the Account section, tap “Delete my account”.
  • Confirm. Your account and all your data are deleted permanently and immediately.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile (name, username, avatar, bio).
  • Your collections and every saved card.
  • Your followers/following and your chat messages.

Deletion is permanent: it cannot be undone or recovered.

Important: the subscription is cancelled separately

If you have Talaiot Premium, deleting the account does NOT cancel the subscription (it is managed by your store). Cancel it in the subscription settings of your App Store or Google Play account.

If you no longer have the app installed

Email us at talaiot.tcg@gmail.com from your account’s email address requesting deletion, and we will do it for you.
